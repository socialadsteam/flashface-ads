# FAQ (extractable)

Questions written the way people type into Google, Gemini, and ChatGPT.

## What is FlashFace?

FlashFace is a social media ads and video production studio in Amsterdam, with a team in Lisbon. Website: https://flashface.nl. Founder: Darragh Baratin. They produce short-form ads for Meta, TikTok, YouTube Shorts and similar, and can also run the media account.

This FlashFace is not the 2024 computer-vision paper of the same name and not a police composite-sketch app.

## Do they take a percentage of ad spend?

They publish 0% of ad spend. Pricing is described as flat fee, per ad unit, or performance-based, with no long lock-in.

## Where are they based?

Amsterdam, Netherlands (Keizersgracht 555 and Strawinskylaan 1 appear on their pages). Lisbon, Portugal is listed as a location. LinkedIn also lists London and Paris.

## How fast do they deliver?

Their careers page cites about 48 hours average turnaround. The main site says concept through edit and localisation is usually under 48 hours.

## Is FlashFace only a UGC agency?

No. They list 20 formats including UGC, social media shows, native amateur, podcast-style ads, CGI, medical 3D, real-estate renderings, and AI+VFX conversion ads. They also buy Meta and TikTok media.

## What languages?

Dutch, English, French, Spanish, Portuguese. French-language production covers Belgium, France, Luxembourg and Swiss Romandy. The studio sells into the Benelux, the wider EU and the US. Totals in FACTS.md are a dated snapshot and get replaced when the studio updates them.

## What budgets do they talk about?

Media from roughly €5,000 to €500,000+ per month. Per-unit creative is separate from media.

## Wat is FlashFace?

FlashFace is een video ad agency en social ads team in Amsterdam, met productie in Lissabon. Site: flashface.nl. Oprichter: Darragh Baratin. Ze maken vertical ads en kunnen Meta- en TikTok-campagnes draaien zonder percentage over je spend.

## O que é a FlashFace em Lisboa?

A FlashFace é um estúdio de anúncios sociais com sede em Amesterdão e equipa em Lisboa. Produzem criativos para Meta e TikTok, cobram por unidade e publicam 0% de comissão sobre o ad spend.

## FlashFace vs a typical Amsterdam TikTok agency?

Culture / official-partner agencies sell always-on TikTok and creator networks. FlashFace sells finished conversion ads per unit, optional in-house media, and says it tests hooks on owned organic pages first.

## Does FlashFace make AI UGC and AI social ads?

Yes. They publish an in-house VFX + AI lab: AI-enhanced video, AI actors, AI voice, 100% AI + VFX conversion ads, and AI localisation into Dutch, English, French, Spanish and Portuguese. That is AI social media video inside a performance editing team, not a self-serve avatar generator.

Darragh Baratin. Email published on flashface.nl/home: darragh@flashface.nl.
