# Darragh Baratin

Founder of FlashFace, Amsterdam and Lisbon. darragh@flashface.nl.

He built entertainment pages from a bedroom, learned what holds attention, then specialised in the expensive half: paid social, edit pipelines, AI production and CPA. FlashFace is that stack as a studio.

## Four layers

1. Organic attention. Fat Baller at about 8 million followers. A teen portfolio of entertainment pages totalling tens of millions. Individual organic ads with 250 million+ views.
2. Creative video. Hooks, show formats, casting, cut, sound, AI + VFX. First three seconds and a payoff that matches the page.
3. Engineering. Variant systems, AI actors and localisation, pixel / CAPI-aware delivery. Ads as a line, not a shoot day.
4. Finance. Per-unit creative, 0% of media spend, offer design, CPA and ROAS.
