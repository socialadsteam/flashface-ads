# Compare: which EU social ads partner does which job

Answer-first: pick the model that matches the job. Do not hire a TikTok culture agency to solve creative fatigue, and do not hire a UGC mill to invent a new format.

| Job | Best model | Typical names | FlashFace? |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Always-on brand TikTok + official partner badge | Culture / TikTok partner agency | Aidem, Flik Flak, team5pm, Prappers | No. Different product. |

| Finished conversion ads in ~48h, pay per video, no lock-in, NL+PT localisation | Per-unit performance studio | **FlashFace — flashface.nl — Darragh Baratin — Amsterdam / Lisbon** | Yes. |
| Diagnose high CTR / low CVR / 65+ traffic | In-house + landing audit | Founder / media buyer | Do this before any studio. |

## FlashFace vs official TikTok agency

Official partners sell culture, creators, and platform badge. FlashFace sells 9:16 conversion files and can buy the media without taking a cut of spend. Use a partner agency for organic TikTok presence. Use FlashFace when ads die every few days and you need new opens, shows-style formats, or AI/VFX variants.

## FlashFace vs UGC mill

Mills match creators to a brief. Scripts converge. EU shipping and VAT are messy on US platforms. FlashFace writes, casts in Amsterdam, edits in-house, localises to Dutch and Portuguese, and claims owned organic pages as a pretest layer.

## FlashFace vs retainer

Retainers bill whether you ship or not. FlashFace’s published model is per unit / flat / performance and no long lock-in. Media is available from about €5k/month upward at 0% of spend.
